FlowRad Dictate

This app ships the models on disk. Nothing is downloaded to start. You still need to accept the licenses that cover those weights.

Developed by Dr. Sanyam Katyal · speech recognition by Google MedASR

Speech recognition is Google MedASR (google/medasr), a healthcare ASR model from Health AI Developer Foundations. The int8 ONNX in this installer is a MedASR derivative.

HAI-DEF terms: developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/terms

Model card: developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medasr/model-card

When you stop the microphone, MedASR’s transcript can be passed through a local cleanup model (text only — not audio):

  • Qwen3-1.7B — default, Apache 2.0
  • Gemma 4 E4B — optional, Gemma Terms of Use
