Speech-to-text — Google MedASR
MedASR (google/medasr) is the only transcriber. Weights ship in the installer (HAI-DEF). Full int8 MedASR — not a distilled student. See ATTRIBUTION.md.
Cleaning — runs when you stop the mic
After MedASR transcribes, Qwen (default) or Gemma 4 edits the text only. Both ship in the installer — no download. Turn Off for the raw transcript.
